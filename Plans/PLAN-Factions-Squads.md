## The Pitch

Survival is harder alone. Lost Spawns supports the full social spectrum - lone wolf, duo, tight squad of friends, organized clan, or player-built faction with territory and ideology. Squads are ad-hoc party groups for immediate cooperation. Factions are persistent organizations with reputation, territory, members, and enemies.

The goal: **every player's best story is about the people they met.** Betrayal, rescue, siege defense, trader handshake, deathbed request, first kiss in the woods, cold-blooded execution. The game provides the stage, systems, and verbs - players write the drama.

## Squad System (Ad-Hoc Party)

### [COMMIT] Forming a Squad

- Walk up to a player, hold interact, **"Invite to Squad"**
- Or add by name/handle via radio/party menu
- Max squad size: 6 by default (server configurable up to 12)
- Squad leader can invite, kick, promote, disband
- Leadership can transfer if leader drops/disconnects

### [COMMIT] Squad HUD

- Squadmate icons and health bars stacked in corner
- Names hover above squadmates in-world (color-coded to squad color)
- Directional arrow if squadmate is off-screen
- Squadmate positions visible on compass band
- Downed status flashes urgently

### [COMMIT] Shared Waypoints

- Squad leader drops waypoint on map, visible to all
- "Ping" system - tag a point, enemy, item, threat (Apex Legends style)
- Ping wheel: Danger, Enemy, Loot, Go Here, Watch This, Need Help
- Voice lines play for ping (player's chosen voice pack)

### [LIKELY] Role Tags

Players pick a soft role when forming squad. Doesn't lock them, just signals intent.

- **Medic** - Heal-focused kit, extra bandages, revive speed bonus via perk
- **Engineer** - Crafting/repair bonus, trap expertise
- **Scout** - Spotter, marker, stealth-focused
- **Heavy** - Tank role, armor, big guns
- **Support** - Ammo, gear, logistics
- **Leader** - Morale buff to nearby squadmates (F76 Inspirational-style)

Roles surface relevant perk cards the player already has equipped - purely a communication tool.

### [LIKELY] Friendly Fire

- **ON by default** in PvP zones
- **OFF** in safe zones (trader towns)
- Server configurable globally
- Squad can toggle internal friendly fire (default OFF within squad, ON outside)
- Accidental squad kill gives negative rep to shooter

### [LIKELY] Shared Stash Permissions

- Squad stash is SEPARATE from personal stash - opt-in, placed at base
- Per-member permissions: Read, Write, Withdraw limited items
- Leader can see withdrawal log (who took what, when)
- Leaving the squad locks you out of the squad stash immediately

### [UNDECIDED] Squad Respawn

- Respawn at squadmate's position if they're in a safe spot (not in combat)
- Costs a shared Squad Token (generated by being in squad, capped at 3)
- Invulnerability window on arrival (3 seconds) to prevent respawn-kill chains

## Faction System (Persistent Organization)

### [COMMIT] NPC Factions (Pre-Built)

Each server ships with a set of pre-built NPC factions, each with:
- Territory (claimed regions)
- Vendors (gear, food, services)
- Quests (bounties, fetch, escort)
- Reputation track (Hostile → Neutral → Friendly → Allied → Trusted)
- Ideology (tells you who they hate and who they love)

**Draft faction roster:**
- **The Settlers** - farming/rebuilding, Neutral to most, hostile to raiders
- **The Wardens** - ex-military, order-focused, hostile to raiders, suspicious of scientists
- **The Wayward** - nomad caravan, traders, neutral to all (if you pay)
- **The Faithful** - religious cult, believes infection is judgment, hostile to mutated players
- **The Carrion** - raider/bandit clan, hostile to most, recruit the desperate
- **The Network** - info brokers, neutral, sells secrets to the highest bidder
- **The Mothfolk** - cryptid worshippers (or cryptids themselves), mysterious

### [LIKELY] Faction Reputation

- Actions raise/lower rep: quests, kills, trades, territory respect, allegiance hits
- Rep unlocks: vendor tiers, quest lines, faction safehouses, faction gear, perk cards
- Two factions can both like you IF they don't hate each other - some are mutually exclusive (Faithful vs. Mothfolk)
- Killing a faction member drops rep with THEM and anyone allied with them
- Turning in a bounty from rival faction boosts your rep with the bounty-giver, tanks it with the target

### [LIKELY] Player-Joinable Factions

- Accept a faction's Oath (a quest chain) to formally join
- Member benefits: vendor discounts, access to faction territory, uniform, rank structure
- Rank progression via rep + quest completion (Recruit → Soldier → Sergeant → Captain → Elder)
- High rank unlocks leadership tools (assign quests to lower ranks, issue bounties)
- Quitting is allowed but has costs (rep collapse, stolen uniform counts as theft)

### [UNDECIDED] Player-Founded Factions

- Found your own faction from scratch with 5+ pledged members
- Pick an ideology, flag, color, territory claim
- Compete with NPC factions for territory control
- Custom tabard/uniform (color + emblem)
- Faction vault, faction stash, faction bank
- Member ranking with customizable titles
- Territory ownership: claim markers, safe houses, respawn beds for members
- Faction war declaration system (see below)

### [LIKELY] Defector Mechanic

- Leaving a faction mid-conflict flags you as a **Defector** for 72 real hours
- Defectors get a bounty from their old faction
- Defectors CANNOT join the rival faction for 24 hours (cooldown)
- Repeated defecting tanks your social score permanently
- Some players will play "eternal defector" - rotating betrayals for profit. Let them. They'll learn.

## Faction Wars

### [COMMIT] Declaration System

- A faction (NPC or player) can declare war on another
- Requires: rep cap with all faction leaders + resource cost + 24h cooldown
- War state: active combat rewards, territory capture mechanics unlocked
- Peace requires: treaty event (signed at a neutral trader town) + resource cost

### [LIKELY] Territory Capture

- Strategic nodes (broadcast towers, fuel depots, water treatment, medical clinic, silo) scattered on map
- Capturing a node flips ownership to your faction
- Owned nodes provide resources (fuel pings, water rations, faction credits)
- Attackers must plant a claim beacon and defend for 10 min (horde will respond)
- Defenders can retake by destroying the beacon

### [LIKELY] War Events

- Wars trigger special dynamic events (see PLAN-Dynamic-World-Events.md)
- Faction convoys to escort/ambush
- Faction leader assassination missions (HIGH rep reward)
- Siege events at faction base
- War broadcasts on radio (see PLAN-Radio-Comms.md) announce objectives

## Social Tools

### [COMMIT] In-Game Chat

- **Local chat** - proximity, voice + text, no range restriction inside safe zones
- **Squad chat** - always-on voice + text to squadmates
- **Faction chat** - same for faction members, works via radio (range/encryption rules apply, see PLAN-Radio-Comms.md)
- **Global chat** - text only, disabled on hardcore servers
- **Whisper** - target a player by name, private text

### [LIKELY] VoIP Quality

- Spatial (positional) proximity voice using WebRTC
- Voice is transmitted over radio for squad/faction (requires radio equipment)
- Radio voice has band-limited audio filter (sounds like a radio)
- Press-to-talk default, toggleable to open mic
- Mute wheel (mute/unmute player or whole channel)

### [LIKELY] Emote Wheel

- 8-slot radial emote menu
- Wave, Point, Come Here, Stop, Thumbs Up, Middle Finger, Sit, Dance
- Custom emotes unlocked via progression
- Emotes have voice lines (player's chosen voice pack)

### [UNDECIDED] Social Board

- Bulletin boards in trader towns
- Players can post: bounties, help-wanted, gear-for-trade, faction recruitment
- Posts cost a small fee (caps inflation)
- Posts expire after 48 real hours

## Squad + Faction Interaction

- Squads form within or across factions - a Settlers scout and a Network info broker can squad up
- Squadmates can STILL PVP each other if friendly fire is on and squad FF is toggled off
- Some factions forbid their members from squadding with rival factions (Faithful players get a rep warning if they squad with Mothfolk)
- Squad leader's ping color = squad color, not faction color (avoids confusion in mixed squads)

## Anti-Griefing

### [LIKELY] Kick/Ban from Squad

- Leader can kick. Kicked player loses squad buffs immediately.
- Kicked player cannot be re-invited for 10 minutes (prevents kick-loop griefing)

### [LIKELY] Rep-Based Squad Visibility

- Players with extremely low social score (see PLAN-P2P-Reputation-System.md) get a **scarlet skull** icon when inviting to squad
- Other players can see the warning and decline invitations
- The game does NOT auto-block low-rep players - player agency - but it surfaces the info

### [UNDECIDED] Lockable Stashes

- Personal stashes can be assigned to a specific squad/faction (prevents the "squad leader raid" exit scam)
- Requires a lock tier (locks are crafted from PLAN-Crafting.md)
- Can be picked with lockpicks (costs time, alerts owner if they're online)

## Player Stories We Want to Enable

- **The Defector** - player swaps factions every week, lives on bounty money
- **The Lone Wolf** - never squads, trades with everyone, feared and respected
- **The Squad That Built a Town** - 6 players claim territory, attract NPCs, become a faction
- **The Rescue** - someone's friend downed behind enemy lines, squad mounts a raid
- **The Double Agent** - faction member secretly feeding intel to rival faction, eventually outed
- **The Last Stand** - squad's base under siege, holds out through the night
- **The Trader's Handshake** - two enemies meet at a neutral town, trade, walk away alive

## Technical Implementation Notes

- **Squad state** lives on the server authority (or P2P host)
- **Faction state** is persistent server data (who's a member, current rep, territory ownership)
- **VoIP** is peer-to-peer WebRTC, spatial audio via Web Audio API (see PLAN-Audio-Design.md)
- **Chat messages** are broadcast via SignalR (server) or WebRTC DataChannel (P2P)
- **Pings** are short lifetime (5s), broadcast to squad only
- **Waypoints** persist until replaced, stored server-side
