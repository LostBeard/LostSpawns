# Third-party content notices

Lost Spawns ships no third-party art or audio. The block, sky, rain and creature textures are painted at start-up from procedural
noise (see `Rendering/BlockTextures.cs`, `SkyTextures.cs`, `CreatureModels.cs`), the creatures are models built from boxes, and the
sounds are synthesised with Web Audio. If CC0 or other licensed content is added later, list its source and license here.
